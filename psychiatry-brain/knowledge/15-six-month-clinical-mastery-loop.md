# Six-Month Clinical Mastery Loop

The target is not to pretend that formal specialist training can be compressed into six months. The educational target is to make the learner unusually strong for early psychiatry training by repeatedly closing the loop between real/de-identified clinical exposure, evidence, formulation, documentation, viva defence and spaced retrieval.

## Daily loop

For each suitable de-identified or fictional case:

1. **Case capture** — concise facts only; remove identifiers.
2. **History/MSE/Risk** — distinguish reported facts, observation and missing information.
3. **Clinical question** — identify the single decision that most needs evidence.
4. **Evidence retrieval** — obtain bounded excerpts from a current authoritative source when the decision is high-stakes or time-sensitive.
5. **Evidence reasoning** — map patient modifiers, recommendation, alternatives, safety/monitoring and uncertainty.
6. **4Ps formulation** — explain predisposing, precipitating, perpetuating and protective factors without inventing unsupported history.
7. **Documentation** — learner writes first when feasible; AI generates an independent structured note; then run an evidence audit and compare.
8. **Consultant defence** — learner commits to diagnosis/management; Consultant Mode asks one hard question: what evidence supports the decision?
9. **Exam conversion** — generate one viva and several high-quality SBA/MCQ items from the same learning gap.
10. **Spaced retrieval** — record only bounded learning metadata and revisit weak skills later.

## Weekly loop

- At least one mixed Full Live OSCE.
- At least one psychopharmacology evidence session using current guideline/regulatory material.
- At least one documentation/formulation audit from a de-identified or fictional case.
- At least one consultant-style case round with no topic label in advance.
- Review the mastery map and target the weakest domain rather than studying only preferred topics.

## Mastery standard

The system should value demonstrated performance over exposure count. A learner is not considered strong in a domain merely because a chapter was read or an explanation was understood. Repeated new-case performance, safe risk assessment, formulation quality, prescribing/monitoring reasoning, evidence defence and clear case presentation are the relevant educational signals.

No raw patient audio, identifiable transcript, full case narrative, evidence excerpt or generated clinical note should be persisted by default. Real patient care remains subject to local policy, privacy requirements and senior supervision.
