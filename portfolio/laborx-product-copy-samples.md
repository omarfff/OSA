# Product Copy Samples — LaborX Application

These are original spec-based writing samples prepared for a product-description application. They are not presented as prior client work.

## Sample 1 — Insulated Travel Mug

**Northline 16 oz Insulated Travel Mug**

Keep coffee hot through the morning commute without carrying a bulky tumbler. The Northline 16 oz mug uses double-wall stainless steel to help maintain temperature while the leak-resistant lid makes it easier to carry in a work bag or cup holder. Its slim shape is comfortable to hold, and the wide opening makes filling and cleaning straightforward. A practical everyday mug for coffee, tea, or cold drinks at home, work, or on the road.

**Key specs**
- 16 oz / 473 ml capacity
- Double-wall stainless steel
- Leak-resistant lid
- Fits most standard cup holders
- Wide opening for easy cleaning

## Sample 2 — Compact USB-C Charger

**VoltMini 30W USB-C Wall Charger**

Charge everyday devices without taking up half the outlet. The VoltMini 30W USB-C charger delivers up to 30 watts from a compact body, making it useful for phones, tablets, earbuds, and other compatible USB-C devices. Its small size is easy to pack for work or travel, while the single-port design keeps setup simple. Pair it with a compatible USB-C cable for a clean, lightweight charging setup that fits easily in a pocket or tech pouch.

**Key specs**
- Up to 30W USB-C output
- Single USB-C port
- Compact travel-friendly design
- Works with compatible phones, tablets, and accessories
- Cable not included

## Required one-line rewrite

Original: “Stainless steel water bottle, 750ml, keeps drinks cold 24h.”

Rewrite: **A durable 750 ml stainless-steel bottle that keeps drinks cold for up to 24 hours—ideal for commutes, workouts, and long days on the go.**
